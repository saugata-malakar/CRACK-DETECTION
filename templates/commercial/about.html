Overview

CrackDetect AI is an enterprise-grade platform for detecting cracks in concrete structures using deep learning. Upload any image of a concrete surface and receive instant analysis including severity classification, measurement estimation, compliance status, and professional repair recommendations.

📊

Dataset: SDNET2018

Trained on the SDNET2018 benchmark dataset:

  • 56,092 images of concrete surfaces (256×256 px)
  • 3 categories: Bridge Decks, Walls, Pavements
  • 2 classes: Cracked and Non-cracked
  • Crack widths from 0.06 mm to 25 mm
